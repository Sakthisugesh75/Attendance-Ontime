One idea, used everywhere

The shift is a grey window, the bar is when someone was actually in, the black line is now. Staff see their own day on the phone; HR sees everyone's stacked as a live register.

Colour only means attendance

Green present, amber late (the hatched part is the late minutes), teal leave, violet work from home, red not in. Buttons and menus stay black and white, so the colours stand out.

Numbers as the headline

Timer and totals use the condensed width of Instrument Sans, so they read from across the room. Everything else is plain sentences: "Came in late", "Waiting for you".
